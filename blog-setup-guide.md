## Prerequisites: What You Need Before Starting

Before setting up AutoniqueAI, ensure you have:

1. **Instagram Business Account** (not personal account)
2. **Facebook Page** connected to your Instagram Business account
3. **Facebook Developer Account** (free to create)
4. **Admin access** to both your Facebook Page and Instagram account

### Converting to Instagram Business Account

If you don't have an Instagram Business account yet:

1. Open Instagram app on your mobile device
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Switch to Professional Account**
3. Select **Business** (not Creator)
4. Connect to your Facebook Page
5. Complete your business profile information

### Step 1: Register for AutoniqueAI

### Step 2: Facebook OAuth Integration

AutoniqueAI uses Facebook's secure OAuth system to connect with your Instagram account:

1. **Click "Connect with Facebook"** on the login page
2. **Grant permissions** for:
   - `pages_messaging`: Send auto-reply messages
   - `instagram_basic`: Access Instagram account information  
   - `instagram_manage_messages`: Receive and send Instagram DMs
3. **Select your Facebook Page** that's connected to Instagram
4. **Authorize the connection**

**Important**: AutoniqueAI only requests minimal permissions necessary for Instagram automation. Your data remains secure and private.

After successful OAuth connection, you'll enter the **Business Onboarding** process:

#### Business Basics

### Step 4: FAQ Setup and Management

The heart of AutoniqueAI is its intelligent FAQ system:

1. **Navigate to FAQ Management** in your dashboard
2. **Add your first FAQ**:
   - **Question**: Common customer inquiry
   - **Answer**: Your desired response
   - **Keywords**: Related terms for better matching

#### Best Practices for FAQ Creation:
- Start with 5-10 most common questions
- Use natural language in both questions and answers
- Include variations of the same question
- Keep answers concise but helpful
- Test responses before going live

#### Example FAQs:
```

A: We're open Monday-Friday 9 AM to 6 PM EST. For urgent inquiries outside business hours, please leave a message and we'll respond first thing in the morning! 🕘

Q: How can I track my order?

Q: Do you offer refunds?

```

## Troubleshooting Common Setup Issues

### Issue 1: "No Facebook Pages Found"
**Solution**: Ensure your Instagram account is connected to a Facebook Page and you have admin access to both.

### Issue 2: "Webhook Connection Failed"
**Solution**: Check that your Instagram account is a Business account (not Personal or Creator).

### Issue 3: "Messages Not Auto-Replying"
**Solution**: 
1. Verify FAQ setup is complete
2. Check that auto-reply is enabled
3. Ensure your FAQs cover the customer's question

### Issue 4: "Permission Denied"
**Solution**: Re-authorize the Facebook connection and ensure all required permissions are granted.
